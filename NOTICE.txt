1. English frequency data — CC BY-SA 4.0
------------------------------------------------
extension/frequency/english-frequency.js incorporates the English 50,000-word
frequency list from FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave:

  Source: https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
  Source file: content/2018/en/en_50k.txt
  Revision: 525f9b560de45753a5ea01069454e72e9aa541c6
  License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  License URL: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The generated list filters punctuation, numbers, markup fragments and other
non-English lexical forms, removes duplicate case-folded tokens, preserves the
remaining order, and encodes the result as an ECMAScript module. The generated
asset is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. FrequencyWords identifies
OpenSubtitles2018 as its corpus source:

  https://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles2018.php

Full attribution: extension/frequency/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt
